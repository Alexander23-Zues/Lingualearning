LinguaKids — mission achievements and class rankings

STUDENT
Open Mga Medalya on the adventure map. My badges and Class ranking are separate
views, with large controls and readable text. Finish every required task in the
mission, including story reading and at least 80% on each quiz, to earn its badge.
A newly earned badge is celebrated when the result reader closes. Historical
badges are not announced again whenever the child opens the application.
Milestones recognize 1, 3, 5 and 10 mission badges. A skipped mission gives no
completion or badge. Repeating the same submission never earns another badge.

RANKING
Each active classroom is scoped to its teacher, grade and real/demo group.
Higher completed-mission count ranks first. If counts tie, the percentage of
current assigned missions completed breaks the tie. Equal values share a rank.
Learners with no completed missions have no rank yet. Placement requiring
teacher reconfirmation is excluded from ranking until it is resolved.
Students and teachers see aliases in the ranking. The student response contains
no other learner's name or ID. Only an authorized teacher can open the linked
student record and identify the child. Rankings are progress indicators, not
quiz grades, reading-ability diagnoses or validated pronunciation scores.

TEACHER
Missions -> Achievements & rankings -> select the assigned grade.
Search by student or alias, choose rows per page, use Previous/Next or Jump to
page, refresh progress, or export the filtered class. CSV includes every matching
page and the teacher's student names/IDs, alias, rank, mission counts, percentage,
badge count and verification status. Keep it private like the quiz gradebook.
Students -> open a record -> Missions also shows retained badges and current rank.

BADGE HISTORY AND CURRICULUM EDITS
The protected ledger keeps one earned badge for each child, grade and mission ID.
It records the original title, difficulty, revision, completion time and evidence
step IDs. Renaming a mission does not rewrite its earned badge. Teacher updates
can change current completion and rank; the historical badge remains earned.
Activity stars stay separate and are never used to inflate mission rankings.

OFFLINE
Prepared laptop collection stores local badges durably with progress and backup.
The local ranking describes only that laptop's roster and is marked pending
cloud verification. A quiz without a local answer key stays pending and cannot
award a mission badge. Export the collection and transfer it online; the cloud
verifies original responses and awards from verified progress. Exported badge
flags, scores and ranking values cannot create trusted cloud achievements.
Downloaded PWA practice content is separate from the laptop collection service.

SECURITY AND CLEANUP
The award ledger is private with RLS and no browser table access. Award writers
cannot be called by anonymous or ordinary authenticated browser roles. Existing
child-session and teacher ownership/grade checks protect the read functions.
Active migration files now match the recorded hosted history. Source variants,
unapplied proposals and the unsafe bulk email-confirmation script are archived
with disabled extensions; do not execute those archived files.
