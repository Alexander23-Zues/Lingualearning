# Curriculum source policy

The system includes management structures and one source-level reference only. It does not seed competency wording or codes.

Authoritative references verified during implementation:

- DepEd MATATAG Curriculum Phase 1 page: https://www.deped.gov.ph/matatagcurriculumk147/
- MATATAG Language Curriculum Guide — Grade 1: https://www.deped.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/MATATAG-Language-CG-Grade1.pdf
- DepEd Learning Portal K to 12 Curriculum Guide: English: https://lrmds.deped.gov.ph/detail/5449
- K to 12 English Curriculum Guide (May 2016): https://www.deped.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/English-CG.pdf

An administrator must record title/version, school-year applicability, exact official learning-area name, page reference, source, reviewer, and review date. Content remains `pending_review` until verified. Educator-created examples remain supplemental and are never presented as official DepEd materials. LinguaKids does not claim DepEd approval or complete curriculum coverage.
